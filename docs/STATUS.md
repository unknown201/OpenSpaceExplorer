# Status

Updated 2026-10-24.

## What works

- Cargo workspace with seven crates (`ose-math`, `ose-data`, `ose-sim`, `ose-net`, `ose-server`, `ose-client`, `ose-importer`). `cargo test --workspace`, `cargo clippy -D warnings` and the wasm check all pass. `trunk build` in `crates/ose-client` produces a loadable page (no game logic in the client, see ADR-0002).
- `ose-math::Orbit`: Keplerian elements <-> state vectors, the original's anomaly solvers and tolerances, propagation, time to true anomaly, true anomaly at radius; 7 tests including elliptic/hyperbolic round trips.
- `ose-sim::bodies`: bodies as ECS entities with `OnRails` orbits, star-space position by walking the parent chain, `main_body_at` (SOI containment). `cargo run -p ose-sim --example kerbol` loads the converted data and prints every body; Kerbin comes out at 13,599,840 km and 9284.5 m/s, Mun at 12,000 km, Minmus at 47,000 km.
- `ose-sim::trajectory`: patched-conic predictor (`predict`): collision, encounter (sampled closest approach refined with the original's halving-step and SOI bisection solvers, 0.01 s tolerance), escape and final patches with handoff via state vectors. Parity: a 100 km Hohmann transfer to the Mun enters its SOI 19,941 s after the burn (the bot's independent estimate: 19,950 s) and, with no lead error, impacts the Mun as the bot predicted.
- `ose-sim::vessels`: vessels on rails as entities; `update_reference_body` re-expresses the orbit around whichever body's SOI contains the vessel (escape and encounter), with tests showing the handoff keeps the star-space position continuous.
- `ose-server` streams the universe: `/ws` session (`Hello` -> `Welcome` + `BodyCatalog`, then `TimeState` + `Snapshot` at 10 Hz with star-space position and velocity of every body, `RequestWarp` sets the warp). The Kerbol system (17 bodies, `assets/own/kerbol-system.json`) is embedded in the binary; `--bodies PATH` overrides. `crates/ose-server/tests/ws_session.rs` spawns the binary and checks the whole session. Protocol version 2 in `ose-net`.
- `ose-sim::Universe` drives vessels: each step advances up to `warp` ticks but never past a vessel's next predicted SOI transition (the original's timescale override), then runs the SOI handoff for every vessel. `spawn_vessel`, `spawn_vessel_in_circular_orbit`, `vessel`, `predict`.
- Powered flight v1 (`ose_sim::flight`, protocol v13): a vessel on the pad or in the air is a point mass integrated every tick in its body's inertial frame with thrust from the current stage (vacuum/sea-level Isp from the craft's staging report), propellant consumption, gravity, atmospheric drag on one reference area (density from the body's pressure and temperature curves), surface contact with crash detection above 12 m/s, co-rotation while landed, staging and attitude holds (up, surface/orbit prograde and retrograde, normal, radial, pitch+heading, fixed vector). `LaunchOnPad` / `LaunchSavedOnPad` put a craft on the KSC pad, `SetControls` and `Stage` fly it, and `VesselState.flight` streams situation, altitude, speeds, throttle, stage, propellant, mass, thrust, TWR and dynamic pressure. Warp is capped at 4 while any vessel is under powered flight; after 10 s of unpowered coasting above the atmosphere the vessel hands off to rails and keeps its flight state (stage, propellant, mass) as `Dormant`. `SetControls` with throttle above zero wakes a dormant vessel at its rails position, so burns from orbit, captures and descents are flown under power; `Stage` works on rails too. Crafts launched straight into orbit (`LaunchCraft`) carry a dormant flight state; the bare per-player probe has none and is refused. A powered vessel crossing a sphere of influence is re-expressed around the new body without a jump. Dormant state is saved and restored. Tests: a two-stage test rocket flies a scripted gravity turn from the pad to a 70 km+ orbit; a stock Kerbal K1 lifts off in the session test; hard landings crash and soft ones do not.
- Part structure (`ose_sim::rigid`, `ose_sim::structure`): every craft launched on the pad or into orbit carries its parts as rapier rigid bodies joined by fixed multibody joints, with the original's joint break limits (`breakingForce` / `breakingTorque` x node factor x 50, size-2+ nodes divided by 3). Each flight step loads the structure with the delivered thrust on the burning stage's engines and the drag on each part; joint force and torque come from Newton-Euler over the subtree each joint carries. A joint over its limit breaks, the pieces away from the root become debris vessels with their mass and propellant (owned by the pilot, `"<name> debris"`), and lost engines scale the stage's thrust. Staging trims the structure to the next stage's parts. Wobble: every non-rigid joint bends with the original's angular spring (`JOINT_RIGIDITY x nodeFactor x 10`, damping 1%) and the moment of inertia of the subtree it carries, driven by the torque the rigid solution puts through it and integrated semi-implicitly in substeps, so boosters on radial decouplers and tall stacks flex under thrust, ring after a sudden load and settle; turning the vessel loads it too (wheel torque at the root, gimbal side force at the engines, tangential and centripetal loads on every part). Joints break on their spring torque, so a ringing joint can fail where a steady one would not. `FlightReadout` (protocol v11) adds `joint_stress` (worst joint load against its limit) and `flex` (each bent part's rotation and offset in the craft frame). Tests: the Kerbal K1 and a small rocket hold under full thrust; the K1 flown broadside at 900 m/s, 3 km up, sheds parts; an overloaded joint splits a craft.
- Drag cubes (`ose_sim::aero`): with a structure, drag is the original's six-face drag cube model per part (tip, surface and tail curves against Mach, the drag-coefficient and Cd-power curves, pseudo-Reynolds, faces covered by stack neighbours removed), turned to `Cd * A` and fed to the point-mass integrator; the reference-area drag remains for vessels without a structure (debris, old saves). The slim catalog keeps each part's stowed cube (packed or retracted shape first). A bare 1.25 m tank nose-on at 100 m/s at sea level draws a few kN; the K1 has about 2.4 m^2 `Cd * A` nose-on and 13 m^2 broadside at Mach 0.3.
- Resource flow graph (`ose_sim::resources`): the original's part flow graph (an edge each way per attachment, crossfeed from the part the edge leaves, decouplers as structure boundaries) with its flow modes: `NoFlow` (solid fuel stays in its booster), `AllVessel`, `StagePriorityFlow` (groups by decoupler distance, nearest first; electric charge, monopropellant, xenon) and `StackPrioritySearch` (tanks reachable over crossfeed; methane, oxidizer). Requests drain group by group, proportionally to each tank's contents inside a group, with one delivered fraction shared by a request's ingredients. A vessel with a structure burns its propellant through the graph per engine (split by rated thrust); its mass, reachable propellant and thrust (engines attached and fed) come from the tanks every step, and staging and breakups drop the actual tank contents. Fuel lines are not modelled yet. Tests: stacked tanks drain at the same fill ratio, the decoupler isolates the upper stage, a starved engine gets the fraction left for both ingredients, the K1 burns boosters and core together and stages the boosters off with their leftover solid fuel.
- Electric charge (`Structure::electric`, rates from the part data: `Data_Command.requiredResources`, `Data_ReactionWheel.RequiredResources` per kN*m, `Data_SolarPanel.ResourceSettings`, `Data_ModuleGenerator.ResourceSetting`, kept in `PartDef.electric` by the importer): solar panels charge by starlight (inverse square against the home world's distance, zero in the shadow of the vessel's body or the planet it orbits; fixed panels at half rating since roll is not simulated), engine alternators while their engine runs and RTGs always; probe cores and reaction wheels (in proportion to how fast the vessel is turning) draw through the flow graph. Wheels without charge give no torque; an uncrewed vessel whose cores have no charge loses control (no steering, throttle cut, `SetControls` refused). Deployable panels count as deployed and transmitters draw nothing yet. `FlightReadout` (protocol v10) adds `electric_charge`, `electric_capacity` and `has_control`.
- Science and the tech tree (protocol v13; `docs/reference-notes/science.md`): the original's 8 experiments, every body's science regions with their baked maps (512 x 256 in `assets/own/science/regions.bin`) and landmark sites, and 87 tech nodes. A vessel's situation (landed, splashed, atmosphere, low or high orbit) and region come from its position; `RunExperiments` runs its parts' experiments where valid (crew and charge permitting) and the reports ride along until `TransmitScience` or `RecoverVessel` (landed on Kerbin, removes the vessel). Values are the original's (`DataValue x body x situation x region` scalars), each report key scores once per campaign, points are submitted reports rounded times `ScienceRewards` plus mission rewards times `MissionRewards`, and `UnlockTechNode` spends them on nodes whose prerequisites are unlocked. In Exploration campaigns the pad refuses crafts with parts the agency has not researched (campaign members only; `LaunchCraft` to orbit stays a dev shortcut). `ScienceState` (capacity, allocated, unlocked nodes, submitted reports, missions) answers `ScienceInfo` and is pushed to everyone when it changes; `/tech-tree.json` and `/missions.json` serve the definitions.
- Missions (`docs/reference-notes/missions.md`): the original's 57 missions with their condition trees, 40 playable with the supported checks (SOI, altitudes, apsides, orbit speed, mass, crew, wheels, parts and part families, activated parts, stored and scored reports, research location, predicted encounters, landings, crashes). The server advances offered missions once a second against the agency's vessels; stage rewards add science points. The first mission completes in a test flight of the K1 to 10 km and back.
- Protocol v9: `FlightReadout.facing` (where the vessel points, unit vector in the body's inertial frame) and `FlightReadout.roll_ref` (the craft's forward side; local north across the axis since roll is not simulated); `ServerMessage::BodyRotation { bodies: Vec<BodySpin> }` right after `BodyCatalog` with each body's `rotation_period_s` and `initial_rotation_deg`.
- `--assets-dir DIR` serves converted game assets (glTF, textures) at `/assets/` with a one-day cache and no index fallback; the service unit points it at `/var/lib/ose/assets` on the container and `install.sh` creates the directory and refreshes the unit when it changes.
- `GET /parts.json` and `GET /resources.json`: the embedded part catalog as `PartInfo` (id, title key, category, size, mass, crew, stage type, `size_m`/`center_m` from the first drag cube or the attach-node bounding box, attach nodes, attach rules, resource containers with capacity and initial units, engines with thrust and vacuum/sea-level Isp, engine/decoupler flags) for thumbnails and the editor, cached one hour; no protocol bump. `SaveCraft` and `LaunchCraft` fill parts that list no resources with their definition containers at `initial_units` (full tanks), so an editor may send empty resource lists. `ose-importer --own-out assets/own` regenerates every committed server asset (bodies, slim parts with bounds, resources, stock craft) in one run.
- Guest sweep: players who never joined the campaign and have been away for `--guest-ttl-s` (default one day) are removed with their vessels and saved crafts every `--guest-sweep-s` (60 s); connected players and campaign members are never swept. Keeps the live server tidy after e2e runs. Tested.
- Craft library (protocol v7): `CraftList` lists the 9 stock crafts (converted by the importer into `assets/own/stock-craft.json`, ids 1..9) plus the player's saved crafts (ids from 1000) with parts, stages, dry/wet mass and delta-v; `SaveCraft`, `DeleteCraft`, `GetCraft` (full document) and `LaunchSavedCraft { id, altitude_m }`. Saved crafts persist in the save file.
- Sessions, difficulty and agency (protocol v6): `ListSessions` -> one shared session (id `shared`, name, mode, UT, last played, players); `SelectSession` joins it; `CreateSession { name, mode, difficulty }` configures it while nobody has joined; `SetAgency { flag_id, colours }` is echoed in `CampaignMember.agency`. `DifficultyOptions` (11 definitions from the imported `DifficultyOptionDefinitions` plus the Easy/Normal/Hard/RocketScientist presets, `assets/own/difficulty.json`) is sent once after `BodyCatalog`. `CampaignState` now carries id, mode, difficulty and `created`.
- Campaign (protocol v5): `JoinCampaign` adds the player to the one shared campaign (idempotent) and `CampaignInfo` reads it; `CampaignState` returns name, start tick, members with join ticks, membership flag and vessel count. Membership lives in the save file.
- Per-stage delta-v (`ose_data::staging::delta_v_by_stage`): stages fire highest id first, decouplers jettison their subtree, engines keep running until jettisoned, each stage burns until the first propellant its engines can reach over crossfeed runs out; Tsiolkovsky with thrust-weighted Isp per stage. Kerbal K2 reads 1375 + 1744 + 3081 = 6200 m/s vacuum over its three burning stages. `CraftLaunched` carries the readout (protocol v4).
- Craft launch: `ClientMessage::LaunchCraft { craft, altitude_m }` validates the craft against the embedded part catalog (`assets/own/parts-catalog.json`, 400 parts with their stowed drag cube and without raw modules, plus `resources.json`), answers `CraftLaunched` with part count, dry and wet mass and a whole-craft vacuum delta-v estimate, and spawns the craft as an owned vessel in a circular orbit (a launch pad replaces this in Phase 6). Session test launches a pod plus Terrier.
- Universe save: time, warp and every vessel (owner, orbit elements, scheduled burn) are written to `--save ose-save.json` every `--save-interval-s` (30 s) and restored at startup, so a server restart keeps the campaign; `--save none` disables. Tested: restart with the same db and save file keeps the player, their single probe, the advanced time and the scheduled burn.
- Accounts for resume continuity: `Welcome.token` identifies the player; a later `Hello` with that token resumes the same player id and keeps their vessels (no duplicate probe). Stored hashed in a small JSON file (`--db ose-accounts.json`, `:memory:` for tests); JSON instead of SQLite so the static Linux server cross-compiles without a C toolchain. Session test covers resume.
- Warp arbitration: each player's `RequestWarp` is a request; the universe runs at the lowest request among connected players, and `TimeState.warp_limited_by` names the player holding it down. Leaving releases the hold. Tested with two clients.
- Burns on rails: `ClientMessage::ScheduleBurn { vessel, ut, prograde, normal, radial }` schedules an impulsive burn in the vessel's orbital frame (`ClearBurn` removes it; `BurnState` answers). The step clamps warp so the burn lands exactly on its tick, applies it, and the streamed `Trajectory` shows the pre-burn patch ending in `Maneuver` followed by the post-burn patches. Test: 841.6 m/s prograde from 100 km gives a 12,000 km apoapsis.
- Protocol v3: on join every player gets a probe in a 100 km Kerbin orbit; the server streams `Vessels` (star-space state, reference body, orbit elements, owner) with every snapshot and `Trajectory` (patched-conic patches with end kind) once a second per vessel. The session test checks both.
- `ose-bot` (ADR-0003): an end-to-end bot that plays the Mun campaign as a client over the protocol. Eleven named steps from server health to a Mun landing, each `PASSED`, `BLOCKED` (feature missing or known server bug, named), `SKIPPED` (no KSP2 data) or `FAILED`. Today 10 pass through the server. The bot sets up a Sandbox campaign, rolls the stock Kerbal K2 out to pad 1, plans and tunes the Mun transfer against the server's prediction, enters the Mun's SOI as predicted, captures into a 41 km orbit, and lands under power at 1.8 m/s. `ascend_to_orbit` is blocked: stage 0 drains the upper stages' tanks through the decouplers, so the bot launches the K2 straight to orbit instead. `cargo test --workspace` spawns a server and checks the frontier in `crates/ose-bot/tests/mun_run.rs`.
- `ose-client` main menu (ADR-0007): the original's menu screen over its own 3D backdrop. `tools/export_menu_scene.py` reads the `kspFlow.unity` scene from the install (the menu Kerbin with its textures, the cloud and starfield cubemaps unwrapped to panoramas, the camera, the lights, the three stock vessels as part placements, the menu sprites) into `assets/web/menu/` and `ui/`; `menu3d.rs` draws it with the WebGPU renderer while no session is joined: Kerbin turning with two cloud layers and an atmosphere rim, one of the three vessels (random, as in the original) spinning in front, the sun and ship fill lights, the starfield around. The renderer gained a fill light and ambient per draw, an atmosphere mode and alpha-blended primitives, and the exporter now writes counter-clockwise triangles (the earlier exports were inside out; the server's assets were re-pushed on 2026-10-24).
- `ose-client` KSC hub (ADR-0007): the original's campus in 3D behind the facility panel. `tools/export_ksc.py` turns `KSC.prefab` into `assets/web/ksc/ksc.glb` (1,093 LOD0 instances of 174 shared meshes, 58 MB, props and lower LODs dropped) and `scene.json` (the original's camera, the facilities as boxes); `ksc3d.rs` draws it with an orbit camera, outlines and names the facility under the pointer, and a click opens the VAB, the launch pad or the Tracking Station (map). - `ose-client` VAB (ADR-0007): the original's assembly hall in 3D (`tools/export_vab.py`, `vab3d.rs`) with the craft drawn from the real part meshes, orbit camera, click to select; picker and craft panel float over it.

## How to run

```
cargo test --workspace
cargo run -p ose-server            # http://127.0.0.1:7777/health
cargo run -p ose-importer          # assets/ksp2/data -> assets/ksp2/converted
cargo run -p ose-bot -- --spawn-server   # play the Mun run against a fresh server, print the step table
cd crates/ose-client && trunk serve
.venv/Scripts/python tools/extract_bundles.py
```

Tooling installed locally: rust 1.99 with `wasm32-unknown-unknown`, trunk 0.22, wasm-bindgen-cli, dotnet 9, ilspycmd 11.1, Python venv in `.venv` with UnityPy.

## Deploy

Live at https://ose.jfly.dev/ (home network and Tailscale). A push to `main` builds the static Linux server and the WASM client in GitHub Actions and ships them by scp to the `ose` container on piper, where `ose.service` runs them (`docs/DEPLOY.md`); `python tools/deploy.py` does the same from a developer machine. `ose-server --static-dir DIR` serves the built client.
